usage: train_wavelet.py [-h] --manifest MANIFEST --fov {3M,6M} --output OUTPUT
                        --axis-order AXIS_ORDER --intensity-scale
                        INTENSITY_SCALE [--depth DEPTH] [--patch Z Y X]
                        [--base-channels BASE_CHANNELS]
                        [--representation {voxel,haar3d}]
                        [--condition | --no-condition]
                        [--batch-size BATCH_SIZE] [--epochs EPOCHS]
                        [--save-every SAVE_EVERY]
                        [--steps-per-epoch STEPS_PER_EPOCH] [--lr LR]
                        [--val-every VAL_EVERY] [--ode-steps ODE_STEPS]
                        [--overlap OVERLAP] [--seed SEED] [--workers WORKERS]
                        [--device DEVICE] [--amp | --no-amp] [--resume]

Train a paired 3D voxel or Haar flow; full-volume validation, checkpoints and
resume.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --manifest MANIFEST
  --fov {3M,6M}
  --output OUTPUT
  --axis-order AXIS_ORDER
                        NPZ axes, e.g. ZYX or YXZ; never inferred
  --intensity-scale INTENSITY_SCALE
                        255 for native uint8, 1 for verified [0,1]
  --depth DEPTH         Resample axial Z only; 0 preserves native depth
  --patch Z Y X
  --base-channels BASE_CHANNELS
  --representation {voxel,haar3d}
  --condition, --no-condition
  --batch-size BATCH_SIZE
  --epochs EPOCHS
  --save-every SAVE_EVERY
                        Keep epoch and best-so-far snapshots every N epochs; 0
                        disables
  --steps-per-epoch STEPS_PER_EPOCH
                        Training batches; random cases with replacement; AMP-
                        skipped updates logged
  --lr LR
  --val-every VAL_EVERY
  --ode-steps ODE_STEPS
  --overlap OVERLAP
  --seed SEED
  --workers WORKERS
  --device DEVICE
  --amp, --no-amp
  --resume              Resume local latest.pt at next epoch
